platform initially focused on acquiring, financing, owning, improving, and
managing Japanese real estate through a formal corporate structure.

Mission:

> Create lasting value through disciplined investment, active stewardship, and
> a thoughtful understanding of place.

> connect across borders, beginning with Japanese real estate.

> intersect. Nexus is the connection that turns those intersections into
